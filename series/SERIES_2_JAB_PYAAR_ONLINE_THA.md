---

## 1. VISION & ROLE
An intimate, realistic, coming-of-age North Indian middle-class romance that tracks a 7-year emotional journey (2020 to 2027).
Not a generic AI romance or over-the-top melodrama. The audience must laugh, feel butterflies, experience doubt, feel the ache of unspoken misunderstandings, heartbreak, and emotional emptiness.

---

## 2. TIMELINE & RELATIONSHIP ARC (2020 – 2027)

* **2020 (Ages 15-16)**: COVID lockdown. Online school classes. Camera turns on. Hesitant "Hi". Daily late-night chats.
* **2021-2022 (Ages 16-18)**: Deep emotional dependence. Virtual study dates. Emotional confession: "I think I'm falling for you."
* **2022-2023 (Ages 17-18)**: First real-life meeting after lockdown. Awkward, real, no cinematic hero-entry. Failure of exam & unconditional emotional support: *"Ek result tumhari aukaat decide nahi karta."*
* **2023-2025 (Ages 18-20)**: College in different cities. Changing priorities, career pressure, family scrutiny. Unspoken distance begins.
* **2025-2026 (Ages 20-21)**: Suspicion, delayed replies, cousin photo misunderstanding, explosive argument, abrupt silence.
* **2027 (Ages 22-23)**: Maturity, bittersweet acceptance, solitary rain, old chat revisited... and an unexpected cliffhanger message.

## 3. CHARACTER BIBLE & VISUAL IDENTITY

### AARAV (Male Protagonist)
* **Age Evolution**: 15–16 (2020) → 22–23 (2027)
* **Personality**: Quiet, observant, slightly introverted, deeply loving, emotionally sensitive, overthinker, loyal, insecure under pressure.
* **Visual Anchor**:
  * Face: Soft youthful jawline in 2020 maturing into structured stubble/clean adult jaw in 2027.
  * Eyes: Deep brown, observant, emotionally expressive.
  * Hair: Natural neat black hair, slightly ruffled schoolboy fringe in 2020 → styled relaxed side-part in 2027.
  * Skin Tone: Warm medium Indian wheatish tone.
  * Style: Simple plain t-shirts/hoodies at home (2020) → casual college shirts → semi-formal dark jacket in café (2027).

### MEERA (Female Protagonist)
* **Age Evolution**: 15–16 (2020) → 22–23 (2027)
* **Personality**: Intelligent, warm, funny, emotionally mature, ambitious, vulnerable, carries family burdens silently.
* **Visual Anchor**:
  * Face: Natural elegant Indian features, warm gentle smile, expressive eyes that betray hidden worry.
  * Hair: Dark wavy brown hair, casual low ponytail/messy bun in online class → open flowing waves in college/later.
  * Distinctive Motif: A delicate silver thread bracelet on her left wrist (appears Ep 1, preserved in memory in Ep 6).
  * Style: Simple pastel kurtis / oversized sweatshirts in 2020 → neat college attire → mature contemporary Indian wear.
