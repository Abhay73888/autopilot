## 🌌 Premise

Across the world, people are experiencing the same phenomenon: **reflective surfaces are moving independently of their subjects.** A decommissioned shortwave radio station in Chernobyl has begun broadcasting again — only at **3:17 AM** — using the voices of whoever is awake, to say their own name back to them.

THE OBSERVER FILES is a found-footage / documentary-style horror series that presents these incidents as real classified intelligence reports, surveillance transcripts, and forensic audio analyses. Each episode escalates the threat level.

---

## 🎬 Characters / Entities

- **The Narrators**: Multiple off-screen documentary voices — cold, forensic, authoritative
- **The Observers**: Unseen entities that exist "on the other side" of reflective surfaces
- **Listener 317**: A recurring unnamed subject whose documented experiences anchor each episode
- **The Frequency 3-1-7**: A shortwave signal at 3.17 MHz that is the communication bridge

---
