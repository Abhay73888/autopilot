## 📜 Series Premise & Creative Brief

**Logline:** Not a monument to genius — a forensic, cinematic investigation into how one undocumented, unlettered Florentine actually *worked*: what he saw, what he drew, what he built, what he failed at, what he never finished, and what later ages invented about him after he was gone.

- **Protagonist / Subject:** Leonardo di ser Piero da Vinci (1452–1519)
- **Narrative Voice:** Deep, authoritative, calm, intellectually forensic, documentary-grade (no hype, no "greatest genius", strictly evidence-based).
- **Core Methodology:** "Omo Sanza Lettere" (Man without letters) — observation over dogma; physical proof over scholastic tradition.

---

## 🏷️ Evidence Badge System

Every claim, scene, and asset in Series 8 carries a verifiable on-screen badge:
- ■ **DOCUMENTED:** Supported by primary or near-contemporary archival record (wills, contracts, municipal tax ledgers, notebook autographs).
- ◆ **SCHOLARLY CONSENSUS:** Broad agreement among leading modern art historians & museum specialists (interpretive, but verified).
- ▲ **HISTORIANS DISAGREE:** Active academic debate (e.g. *Salvator Mundi* attribution, certain anatomical dates, sitter alternatives).
- ✕ **LEGEND / MYTH:** Later unverified fable or modern folklore (e.g. Verrocchio dropping brush, death in Francis I's arms, mirror-writing master code).
- ◇ **MODERN RECONSTRUCTION:** Physical replica, 3D CGI simulation, or modern mechanical test (strictly labeled on-screen).
